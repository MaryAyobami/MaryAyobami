## About Me

I am a Computer Science Ph.D. student. My work sits at the intersection of computer systems, security, and performance.

I care about two related kinds of trust in computer systems: trusting that workloads remain protected while they run, and trusting that reported performance results will hold beyond a particular experimental setup.

My current projects involve:

- 🔐 Confidential computing and remote attestation
- 📊 Performance variability and hardware sensitivity

These projects currently involve Intel SGX and TDX, Gramine, Keylime, Linux IMA, Open5GS, and CloudLab.
